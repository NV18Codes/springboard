A Legacy of Execution Excellence

The Story Behind SpringboardPro and the Vision That Drives It

After over two decades of leadership roles across large global enterprises and high-growth organisations, where she established enterprise PMOs, led business transformations, scaled operations, and steered 3,000+ projects — Cecilia D'Silva recognised a universal challenge: strategies, though strong, often stumbled in execution.

That insight sparked the founding of SpringboardPro, a firm built to bridge the gap between ambition and tangible outcomes.

An award-winning professional, Certified Independent Director, PMP, and former Senior Vice President – PMO, Cecilia brings a distinctive blend of governance discipline, strategic clarity, and people-centric leadership.
