Founder's Story

India PMO Leader of the Year 2022 • Certified Independent Director • PMP

“Execution is where strategy becomes reality.”

With over 25 years in leadership roles across HSBC, Oracle, DHL, and Kinara Capital, Cecilia D’Silva has built enterprise PMOs, driven business transformation, steered large-scale operations, and delivered 3,000+ projects across industries. Through these experiences, she observed a recurring challenge: strategies, though strong, often stumbled in execution.

Cecilia founded SpringboardPro to bridge that gap. An award-winning professional, Certified Independent Director, Project Management Professional (PMP), and former Senior Vice President – PMO at Kinara Capital, she blends strategic foresight, governance discipline, and people-centric leadership to deliver measurable impact.

Her approach is defined by an ability to simplify complexity, inspire teams, build trust, and transform how organisations execute at scale — enabling leaders to move with clarity, confidence, and disciplined execution powered by governance, automation, and data-driven insights.

SpringboardPro embodies her belief that execution excellence is a powerful competitive advantage — and that every organisation deserves a trusted partner to turn intent into outcomes with clarity and confidence. For Cecilia, SpringboardPro is more than a consulting firm; it is a launchpad for sustainable growth, collaboration, and success.
