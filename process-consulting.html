Why Process Consulting Matters

Most execution challenges do not stem from lack of capability or intent. They arise because processes are unclear, disconnected, or overly dependent on individuals.

Without well-defined processes:
  • Decisions slow down or escalate unnecessarily
  • Accountability becomes ambiguous
  • Handoffs break between teams
  • Outcomes vary based on people, not systems
